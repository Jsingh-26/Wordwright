# Hero illustration brief

Used on the README, installer and About page. Not used as the app icon (too detailed for 16 px).

**Idea:** a maker at work. One continuous line runs left to right: rough and jagged where it starts, clean and smooth after it passes under a fountain-pen nib held by a hand. Rough text in, shaped text out.

**Rules (craft-studio):** flat illustration; Forge ink `#23408E` on white with one Ember `#C7621E` accent at the nib; bold simple shapes; no stars, sparkles or glow; no background grid or pattern; no text inside the illustration.

**Free tool:** Vistaprint AI Logomaker (free SVG/PNG, no watermark). Paste:

> A hand holding a fountain pen, drawing one horizontal line. The line starts messy and jagged on the left and becomes a clean smooth stroke to the right of the nib. Flat illustration, deep ink blue on white, small orange accent at the nib, bold simple shapes, no stars or sparkles, no background pattern, no text.

Generate several, pick one, then recolour to the exact palette in Inkscape (free) and save as `brand/hero.svg`.
